Kind: software

Product: a birthday reconstruction app for people who want a factual view of the date and place they were born.
Primary action: Rebuild my birth day.

Facts from the reference task:
- A date is required; place and time refine weather and sky calculations.
- Weather comes from public historical data when available.
- Historical events and notable births come from public data.
- Private birth details stay in the browser.
- A salted fingerprint can be recorded on BOT Chain without publishing the date, time or place.
- A birthday reconstruction can be gifted to another wallet.

Voice: technical-curious; plain facts first; concise and precise; no nostalgia slogans; the product names what it computes and what stays private.

Screens:
1. Hero — category, hard fact, birth-input console, one screen.
2. App frame — real reconstruction report UI, one screen.
3. Data path — live diagram from input to weather/history/sky, one screen and one dealt moment.
4. Keepsake — wallet and salted fingerprint UI, one screen on contrasting ground.
5. FAQ and close — privacy, limits, and main action, one screen.

Name search is recorded in names.json.

Design deal:
- Palette: soot / white / pale lime (`#0f0f0f`, `#ffffff`, `#dafa9c`), deliberately separate from the source site's purple-pink celestial styling and the earlier orange/pink builds.
- Typography: Instrument Sans with DM Mono for data and controls.
- Hero: triptych composition with a timed product-UI collage; the action is a full-height panel rather than a conventional centered CTA.
- Navigation: compact card navigation; footer: statement close.
- Rhythm: dark technical product screens interrupted by one white data-path screen.
- Dealt moment: an iris-open transition reveals the data path only; it explains how one private input fans out to three public sources. Motion is disabled for reduced-motion and small-screen users.
