Read this before you keep a record.

What leaves my browser?

The selected date and coordinates are sent to public weather and history services to build the report. If you use BOT Chain, only a salted fingerprint, an optional public year, and any public gift note are sent on-chain.

Can Bornora recover my birth details?

No. The contract stores no raw date, time, or place. Keep the downloaded private backup if you want to verify the same fingerprint later.

Why might an old date have no weather?

Historical coverage depends on the place and date. Bornora leaves unavailable fields clearly marked.

Which wallets work?

Bornora discovers EIP-6963 wallets, multi-provider browser extensions, and standard injected wallets. Mobile users also get a MetaMask deep-link option.
